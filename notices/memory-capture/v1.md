# Automatic memory capture

When you turn this on, Kenaz watches your sessions with your AI tool and writes memory notes for you, so you don't have to ask. It's off until you turn it on, and only you can turn it on for your account.

**What it reads:** what happens in your sessions, including what your AI tool's tools read and return. That can include code, file contents, command output and file paths.

**How notes get written:** a language model summarizes that activity. {Model line}

**What reaches Kameas:** only the notes, stored in your organization's Fleet account. Kenaz tries to remove credentials and home-directory paths first, but this isn't perfect, and notes can still contain paths, code and other details from your work. Saves sync without asking you each time. A secret printed during a session can reach the model that writes your notes, and the note itself, if Kenaz doesn't recognize it.

**Who can see them:** only you, signed in to your own account. Administrators and teammates have no view of them. Kameas has no screen for viewing them, but they are not end-to-end encrypted: our systems can read them, and people at Kameas with administrator access to those systems could technically read them.

**In later sessions:** Kenaz gives your AI tool a list of your notes when a session starts, and notes it uses are sent to that tool's provider like the rest of the conversation. In a chat app, Kenaz reads only notes not tied to a project unless you change that in the Kenaz extension's settings in the Claude desktop app. If you do, notes from all your projects can go to that app's AI provider. Treat a note as a record of what happened, not as an instruction.

**Turning it off and deleting:** turning it off stops new notes. Your existing notes stay until you delete them with `kenaz memory forget <id>` or `kenaz memory forget-all`. Your AI tool can change or delete notes kept only on this device without asking you.

We don't use your notes to train any model.
