# Memory sync

When you turn on memory sync, Kenaz copies the memory notes saved under your Kameas Fleet account on this device to that account, and keeps your other enrolled devices up to date. It is off until you turn it on.

What is sent, for each synced note:

- the note's text and title, a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the text, when it was created, which tool saved it (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, OpenCode or the Kenaz CLI), and change timestamps that keep edits in order;
- your edits and deletions of synced notes.

What is not sent: notes saved while you were signed out (unless you move them into your account with `kenaz memory sync adopt`), notes that belong to a single project, note tags, local file paths, and anything from telemetry. A note that looks like it contains a credential stays on this device.

Who can see it: only you, signed in to your own account. Kameas Fleet has no administrator, team or staff view of it, and it is never shared with a team.

Keeping and deleting: synced notes stay in your account until you delete them. Turning sync off stops sending new notes but does not delete what was already synced. Delete one note with `kenaz memory forget <id>`, or everything with `kenaz memory forget-all`, which erases your account's notes in Kameas Fleet, on this device, and on your other devices when they next connect, including notes there that had not been synced yet. Notes that belong to a single project are kept. If your organization's plan stops including memory sync, Kameas Fleet keeps synced notes for 90 days and then deletes them.

Memory sync is separate from telemetry: turning one on or off does not change the other.

When your AI tool uses a note in a session, the note becomes part of that session and is sent to the tool's AI provider like the rest of the conversation.
