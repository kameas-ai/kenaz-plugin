# Memory sync

When you turn on memory sync, Kenaz copies the memory notes saved under your Kameas Fleet account on this device to that account, and keeps your other enrolled devices up to date. It is off until you turn it on.

What is sent, for each synced note:

- the note's text and title, a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the text, when it was created, a short label for the app that saved it (for example `claude-code`, `codex` or `cli`), and change timestamps that keep edits in order;
- the project a note belongs to, where it has one, shown by its name (the name of its folder, unless you change it);
- your edits and deletions of synced notes.

What is not sent: notes saved while you were signed out (unless you move them into your account with `kenaz memory sync adopt`), note tags, and anything from telemetry. Kenaz doesn't send a separate record of your file paths, but a note's text can include paths and other details from your work. A note that looks like it contains a credential stays on this device.

Who can see it: only you, signed in to your own account. Kameas Fleet has no administrator, team or staff view of it, and it is never shared with a team. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our systems can read it, and people at Kameas with administrator access to those systems could technically read it.

Keeping and deleting: synced notes stay in your account until you delete them. Turning sync off stops sending new notes but does not delete what was already synced. Delete one note with `kenaz memory forget <id>`. Delete everything with `kenaz memory forget-all`. It permanently erases every note saved under your account, including project and session notes, in Kameas Fleet, on this device, and on your other devices. That includes notes on those devices that never synced and exist nowhere else. Your other plugin devices erase these notes when they next sync, which may be later if sync is off there or your organization's plan is paused or has lapsed. A device running the Kenaz Harness keeps the notes on it for now. A device that hasn't synced before, or has a wrong clock, could send a note back to Kameas Fleet; you can delete it with `kenaz memory forget` or by asking your AI tool to forget it. Notes saved while you were signed out aren't affected. Erased notes can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days. This can't be undone, so export your notes first if you may want them. If your organization's plan stops including memory sync, Kameas Fleet keeps synced notes for 90 days and then deletes them.

Memory sync is separate from telemetry: turning one on or off does not change the other.

When your AI tool uses a note in a session, the note becomes part of that session and is sent to the tool's AI provider like the rest of the conversation. In a chat app, Kenaz reads only notes not tied to a project unless you change that in the Kenaz extension's settings in the Claude desktop app. If you do, notes from all your projects can go to that app's AI provider.
